Our story

NK Journeys began with a simple frustration. Planning a trip in India usually means comparing a dozen tabs, decoding package lists that never say what is actually included, and hoping the hotel matches the photos.

We wanted one place where the plan, the price and the people behind the trip are all clear before you pay. So every package we list follows the same pattern: a day-by-day plan you can read in two minutes, a plain list of what is and is not included, and a price with GST shown upfront.

We are a small team, and we like it that way. The people who plan your trip are the people you can call when something changes.

How we plan a trip

The same six steps for a two-day pilgrimage or a week in the Himalaya.

1

We listen

You tell us who is travelling, when, and what a good trip means to you: slow mornings, long drives, food, temples or snow.

2

We shortlist

We suggest one or two routes from our packages, or adjust a package to fit your dates and pace.

3

We check

Hotels, drivers and timings are confirmed before we quote. If a leg is too rushed, we say so and change it.

4

We price it plainly

You see the trip fare, GST and what is included or not, in one summary. No surprises at checkout.

5

We brief you

Before you leave you get pickup details, a document checklist and local tips for the season.

6

We stay reachable

Someone on our team is a call or message away for the whole trip, not only office hours on day one.

What we stand for

Clear prices

You see the fare, the GST and the total before you pay. Cancellation terms are written in plain language and linked from every package.

Safety first

We avoid rushed mountain drives, brief you on altitude and weather, and keep a contact on call during every trip.

Local first

We prefer family-run stays, local guides and neighbourhood food, so the places you visit benefit from your visit.

Travelling responsibly

India's most loved places are also its most fragile. We ask every traveller to carry a refillable bottle, avoid single-use plastic, dress modestly at temples, ask before photographing people, and keep to marked trails in the mountains. Where we can, we book hotels that manage waste and water carefully.

If a place is overcrowded in a given season, we will tell you and suggest a quieter week or a nearby alternative.
