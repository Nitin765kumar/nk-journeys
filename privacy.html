1. Who we are

NK Journeys is a travel planning service that lets you browse trips across India, make bookings and contact our team. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean NK Journeys, and "you" means anyone who uses our website.

2. What we collect

We collect only what we need to plan and confirm a trip:

  • Contact details: name, mobile number, email address and city.
  • Traveller details: names of everyone travelling, travel dates and the number of adults and children.
  • Booking and payment records: booking ID, payment ID, amount, payment method and the last four digits of a card. We never store a full card number, CVV or UPI PIN.
  • Messages you send us through the contact form.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, to keep the site working.

3. How we use it

  • To confirm bookings, issue vouchers and share pickup and hotel details.
  • To arrange services with hotels, drivers and guides for your trip.
  • To answer your questions and handle changes, cancellations and refunds.
  • To meet tax and accounting rules.
  • To improve the website. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Where your data is stored in this demo

This site is a demo project with no server. Bookings and enquiries are saved only in your own browser using local storage, and are not sent to us or anyone else. You can remove them at any time by clearing the site data in your browser settings. A live service would store this data on a secured server and describe it here.

5. Who we share it with

We share only what is needed to deliver your trip:

  • Hotels and transport partners, for check-in and pickups.
  • Our payment gateway, which processes your payment and holds card details so we do not have to.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use local storage to remember your booking while you move through the checkout and to keep your voucher. We do not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics later, we will list them here.

7. How long we keep data

Booking and payment records are kept for as long as tax and accounting rules require. Enquiries that do not lead to a booking are deleted after 12 months.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, subject to legal record-keeping duties. Under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may also withdraw consent and raise a complaint. Contact us using the details below.

9. Security

We use reasonable safeguards: encrypted connections, payments handled by a certified gateway, and limited access to booking data. No system is perfectly secure, so please also protect your own devices and never share your booking ID publicly.

10. Children's data

We collect children's names and ages only to arrange their travel, and only from a parent or guardian making the booking.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update the date at the top of this page when the policy changes. Significant changes will be announced on the booking page.

12. Contact us

Questions about privacy? Write to hello@nkjourneys.example or use our contact form.
