1. Accepted methods

You can pay by UPI, debit or credit card, net banking or wallet. We do not accept cash, cheques or transfers to personal accounts. Never pay anyone who contacts you claiming to represent us outside this website.

2. Currency and tax

All payments are in Indian rupees. GST at 5% is shown in your booking summary before you pay.

3. Pay in full or pay 30% now

At checkout you can pay the full amount, or pay 30% now to secure your dates. The balance is due at least 7 days before departure. We will remind you before the due date.

4. Coupons

A coupon reduces the trip fare, before GST, and can be used once per booking. Coupons cannot be combined or exchanged for cash, and are not refunded separately.

5. Failed payments

If a payment fails, your booking is not confirmed and you are not charged. If money leaves your account but you see a failure message, the bank or gateway normally reverses it within 5 to 7 working days. Keep your payment ID and contact us if it does not return.

6. Card and payment security

Card and UPI details are entered on the payment gateway's secure page in a live service, and we never see or store them. On this demo site no real payment is made, so please do not enter real card details.

7. Receipts and vouchers

After a successful payment you receive a booking ID, a payment ID and a voucher you can print. Keep them until the trip ends.

8. Refunds

Refunds follow our cancellation and refund policy and go back to the original payment method.

9. Disputes

If you think a charge is wrong, contact us first with your booking ID and payment ID. We will respond within one working day and work to resolve it quickly.
