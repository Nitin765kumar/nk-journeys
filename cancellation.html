Cancellation and refund policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026. This is sample text written for a demo project. It is not legal advice, and a real business should have its policies reviewed by a lawyer.

1. Changing your date

You can move your trip to another date once, free of charge, up to 15 days before departure, subject to availability and any difference in fare. Later changes count as a cancellation.

2. Cancelling a booking

Refunds are calculated on the amount you have paid, counted from the day we receive your cancellation request until the departure date:

When you cancelRefund of amount paid
30 days or more before departure90%
15 to 29 days before departure70%
7 to 14 days before departure40%
Less than 7 days before departure, or no-showNo refund

GST is refunded in the same proportion.

3. Non-refundable items

Some costs cannot be recovered once incurred, and are deducted before any refund: flight, train or permit tickets already issued, entry tickets booked in your name, and payment gateway charges.

4. If you paid only the 30% advance

The same schedule applies. If the refund due is less than the advance, the difference is kept. If you have not paid the balance by 7 days before departure, we may cancel the booking, and the cancellation schedule applies.

5. If we cancel

If we cancel a trip for any reason other than your own conduct, you can choose a new date at no extra cost, or a full refund of everything you paid.

6. Events beyond our control

If weather, landslides or government orders stop a trip during travel, we will rearrange it where possible. Costs for services already used cannot be refunded, but unused, prepaid services will be refunded or credited after the partner confirms.

7. How to request a cancellation

Write to hello@nkjourneys.example with your booking ID, or use our contact form. The date and time we receive your message counts as the cancellation date.

8. When you get your refund

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days after we approve them. Banks may take a little longer to show the credit.
