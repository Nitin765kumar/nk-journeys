How to book, step by step

  1. Choose a trip. Open a package and read the day-by-day plan, what is included and what is not.
  2. Pick your date and travellers. Choose a date at least 7 days ahead. Children aged 5 to 11 pay half fare.
  3. Enter names exactly as on ID. Hotels, permits and entry tickets are issued in these names, so a spelling mistake can cause trouble at check-in.
  4. Use a mobile number and email you check. Booking updates and pickup details are sent there.
  5. Apply a coupon if you have one. In this demo, NKJ10 gives 10% off the trip fare.
  6. Choose how to pay. Pay in full, or pay 30% now and the balance 7 days before departure.
  7. Complete the payment. If it fails, you are not charged. Try again or choose another method.
  8. Save your voucher. It shows your booking ID, payment ID and travellers. Print it or keep a screenshot.

Before you travel

Documents

  • A valid photo ID for every adult. Carry the original, not only a photo on your phone.
  • Your booking voucher, printed or saved offline.
  • Age proof for children.
  • For Ladakh and some border areas, extra permits may be needed. We tell you if they apply to your plan.

Packing by trip type

  • Mountains: layers, a windproof jacket, sunscreen, sunglasses and sturdy shoes.
  • Beaches: light cotton clothes, a hat, sunscreen and a quick-dry towel.
  • Heritage and spiritual: modest clothes that cover shoulders and knees, and shoes that are easy to remove.

Altitude

Above about 3,000 metres, take the first day slowly, drink plenty of water and avoid alcohol. If you get a severe headache, breathlessness or dizziness, tell your driver or guide at once and descend. Speak to your doctor beforehand if you have a heart or lung condition.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change my travel date?

Yes. You can change the date once, free of charge, up to 15 days before departure, subject to availability. Later changes are treated as a cancellation and a new booking. See the cancellation policy.

What if my payment fails?

If the payment fails, no money is taken for this booking. If your bank shows a debit and we show a failure, the amount is normally reversed by the bank within 5 to 7 working days. Keep your payment ID and contact us if it does not return.

Are flights or train tickets included?

No. Packages start and end in a named city, and you reach that city yourself. We can suggest timings that fit the first and last day of the plan.

Can you customise a package?

Yes. Use the contact form to tell us your dates, group size and budget, and we will suggest changes such as a different hotel category, an extra day or a different pace.

Is the trip suitable for children and older travellers?

Most plans are. Some, such as Ladakh, involve high altitude and long drives, so check with your doctor first. Tell us before booking and we will suggest a gentler route if needed.

How many people can travel together?

Online booking supports up to 8 adults and 4 children at a time. For larger groups, send us an enquiry.

What ID do travellers need?

Every adult needs a valid government photo ID. Children need an age proof such as a school ID or birth certificate. Foreign nationals need a passport and valid visa.

How do I get a copy of my voucher?

Open the track-booking form on this page, enter your booking ID and use the voucher link. In this demo, bookings are stored only in the browser where you made them.

Still need help?

Our team replies to messages within one working day. For urgent help during a trip, use the number on your voucher.
